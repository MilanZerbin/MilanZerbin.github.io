## Nancy Hingston Celebration

In the last two days of October 2025, i attended a conference held in Copenhagen to celebrate Nancy Hingstons 60th Birthday. 

Next to it being right in theme with me learning some String Topology, a main focus of many of the talks, it was a heartwarming event, even culminating in singing a birthday song together.

You can check out the song and more details on the [conference website](https://www.math.ku.dk/english/calendar/events/nancy-hingston-celebration/).
