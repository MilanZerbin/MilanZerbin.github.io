## How good are off the shelf LLMs at bachelor level mathematics?

In summer 2025, i organized [this seminar](/teaching/aisem) on the topic of *AI in Math*.
One of the parts of the seminar was experenting how to efficiently solve bachelor level math problems with at the time off the shelf LLMs.

### Results

Our little experiment (sampling from over 300 exercises of a classical Analysis 1-3 course) resulted in:

 - Of the Shelf LLMs at the time solve around 30% of the posed exercises perfectly on first try. 37% more are almost perfectly solved.
 - In ~40% of the cases, a second try at the LLM finds all or most mistakes present in the solution.
 - In 20% of the cases, nonexisting errors were hallucinated upon asking.
 - A slighly more powerful agentic system our student Jörn Stöhler built was already able to perfectly solve almost all posed bachelor level exercises.

If you wish to take a look at the quality of solutions that the agentic system came up with, [here](/teaching/kisem25/expl1.pdf) and [here](/teaching/kisem25/expl2.pdf) are two example average solutions.
