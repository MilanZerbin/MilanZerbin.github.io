## Spaces of Psc-Metrics on nonorientable Manifolds

In the end of April 2025, i gave a talk on [my result](activities/masterthesis.md) on the homotopy type of spaces of positive scalar curvature metrics on nonorientable manifolds at the geometry seminar of the [ICMAT]() in Madrid.

The invitation, which i am most thankful for, was extended to me by [Angel Gonzales Prieto](), who i wish to thank also for inspiring discussions about topological quantum field theories.

You can find the slides of the talk [here](/pres/slidesmadrid.pdf).
