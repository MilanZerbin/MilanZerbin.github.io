## Lecture: Differential Topology

In the winter term 25/26 I was the teaching assistant for the course *Differential Topology and the h-Cobordism Theorem* taught by [Filip Brocic](https://sites.google.com/view/filip-brocic/home).

The course was mainly following [Milnors Book](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~hekmati/Books/Milnor2.pdf) on the h-Cobordism theorem. 
For the full plan of the course, see [the course announcement](/teach/difftop25/announcement.pdf) we posted.
