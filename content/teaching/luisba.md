## Dividing curves and convex surfaces in contact 3-manifolds

Recently, i had the opportunity to mentor my first Bachelor Student: Luis Lohse.
The idea for his thesis project on dividing curves and convex surfaces in contact 3-manifolds resulted from a lecture given by Kai Ciliebak on H-principles in contact geometry one semester prior.
We noticed, that there is no detailed accesible proof in the literature of the classic theorem of Giroux that every characteristic foliation generated by a Morse-Smale vectorfield is convex.

The goal of this thesis was to:

 - Give a much more detailed version of Giroux's original proof
 - Discuss whether the unproven claim, that almost-Morse-Smale vectorfields suffice in the proof, is backed

While the first goal was accomplished, time did not permit chasing the second one in the contents of the thesis.
You can find the finished thesis in german [here](), Luis handed it in on March 06, 2026.

### Future Goal 

We will at some point jointly make the detailed proof accessible in english and extend it to the almost-Morse-Smale case.
