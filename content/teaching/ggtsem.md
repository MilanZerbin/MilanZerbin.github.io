## Seminar: Geometric Group Theory

In the summer term 24 [Georg Frenck](http://frenck.net/Math/) and I hosted a Bachelor seminar on geometric group theory following mostly the [book by Clara Löh](https://loeh.app.uni-regensburg.de/ggt_book/ggt_book_draft.pdf)

For the full idea of the course, see [the course announcement](/teaching/sem24/announcement.pdf) and the [seminar plan](/teaching/sem24/plan.pdf), which are in german.
